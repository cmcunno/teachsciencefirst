Hooke's Law - GCSE Physics Guide

Hooke's Law Statement

"The extension of an elastic object, such as a spring, is directly proportional to the force applied, provided that the limit of proportionality is not exceeded."

F = k · e
F = Force (N)
k = Spring constant (N/m)
e = Extension (m)

Elastic Deformation

When the applied force is removed, the spring returns to its original natural length ($L_0$). The material behaves elastically below the elastic limit.

Inelastic (Plastic) Deformation

Beyond the limit of proportionality (P), the spring remains permanently stretched even when weights are unloaded.

Gradient & Elastic Energy

  • On a Force against Extension graph, the straight line gradient equals the spring constant $k$: gradient = ΔF / Δe = k.
  • The area beneath the line equals the elastic potential energy stored ($E_e$) in Joules: E_e = ½ · k · e² = ½ · F · e.
