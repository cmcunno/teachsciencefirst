🧪 Enzyme-Controlled Reaction: Trypsin & Casein

Expanded View
📋 Method Synopsis ⛶

Step 1: Select the temperatures you want to test (20°C, 30°C, 40°C, 50°C, 60°C or enter custom values).

Step 2: Set up test tubes with milk powder solution, trypsin enzyme, and pH 7 buffer at each selected temperature.

Step 3: Incubate tubes in water baths to equilibrate to target temperature.

Step 4: Mix enzyme with substrate and immediately start timing. The white casein protein makes the solution cloudy.

Step 5: As trypsin digests casein, the solution becomes clearer. The colorimeter measures this as decreasing absorbance (1.00 = cloudy, 0.30 = clear).

Step 6: Record time taken to reach minimum absorbance - this is your reaction time.
