Notes

  1. Resting potential (−70 mV): Na⁺/K⁺ pump moves 3 Na⁺ out, 2 K⁺ in (ATP). Membrane more permeable to K⁺, which diffuses out.
  2. Generator potential: a stimulus opens Na⁺ channels; Na⁺ diffuses in.
  3. Threshold (−55 mV): voltage-gated Na⁺ channels open and Na⁺ floods in: depolarisation (+40 mV).
  4. Repolarisation: Na⁺ channels close; voltage-gated K⁺ channels open, K⁺ out.
  5. Hyperpolarisation: K⁺ channels slow to close (≈ −80 mV). Then −70 mV is restored (refractory period).
